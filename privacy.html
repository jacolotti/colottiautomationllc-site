Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how Colotti Automation LLC handles information provided through this website. It applies to colottiautomationllc.com.

Information you provide

When you submit the contact form, we receive the information you enter, which may include your name, email address, company name, phone number, project location, type of support requested, urgency, and message. Some fields are optional. Please do not submit passwords, confidential PLC programs, or other sensitive information through the general inquiry form.

How information is used

We use inquiry information to review requests, respond to questions, discuss potential services, prepare proposals, and communicate about requested work. We may retain correspondence as needed for business administration, recordkeeping, and applicable legal obligations.

Form processing and spam prevention

Our contact form uses Formspree to receive and route submissions. Cloudflare Turnstile helps check submissions for automated abuse. These providers may process technical information associated with a submission, such as IP address, browser or device signals, and verification data, under their own privacy terms. You can review the providers' policies at Formspree and Cloudflare.

Website hosting and third-party content

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. It may link to external websites and embed third-party content, including YouTube videos. Visiting external content may involve data processing by those providers under their own policies. Technical access logs may be handled by hosting and infrastructure providers.

Analytics

Website analytics or measurement tools may be used to understand site usage and improve the website. Such tools may process information about visits, pages viewed, and browser or device characteristics. Any analytics settings and retention practices are governed by the applicable service configuration.

Sharing and retention

We do not publish contact-form inquiries as customer testimonials without permission. Information may be shared with service providers that support website operation, form processing, or business communications, or when required by law. Inquiry information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above and applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Your choices and questions

You can request information about, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have submitted, subject to applicable requirements and legitimate business recordkeeping needs. To make a privacy-related request, contact Colotti Automation LLC through the website contact form and identify it as a privacy inquiry.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as website services or information practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are made.
